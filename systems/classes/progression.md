## Classes and progression {#classes-and-progression}

**Proficiency bonus** by tier (used on d20 tests): Tier 1–2 **+2**, Tier 3–4 **+3**, Tier 5–6 **+4**. See [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md).

All characters start as one of the Base Classes, based on your starting attribute scores.  
Through accomplishing specific deeds, surviving dangerous situations, and accumulating experience, characters can progress to Intermediate Classes.  
Advancing to Advanced Classes requires not just experience, but also completing specific quests, gaining rare knowledge, or being recognized by powerful entities.  
Legendary Classes are the stuff of myths \- perhaps only one or two exist in all of Aventhar at any given time.

Tiers:  
1 (Starting): Peasant, Laborer, Urchin, Apprentice, Militia, Novice  
2 (Experienced): Footman, Scout, Thief, Acolyte, Hedge Wizard, Merchant  
3 (Skilled): Warrior, Ranger, Rogue, Priest, Mage, Bard  
4 (Expert): Knight, Hunter, Assassin, Cleric, Sorcerer, Diplomat  
5 (Master): Paladin, Beast Master, Shadowblade, High Priest, Archmage, Loremaster  
6 (Legendary): Eternal Champion, Primal Hunter, Shadow Walker, Avatar of the Gods, Ether Lord, World Shaper

**Machine-readable promotions:** [`data/deeds/promotions.json`](../../data/deeds/promotions.json) · [deeds.md](deeds.md)

Progression Requirements:

Tier 1 to 2:  
Footman: Survive 10 combats; Master 2 weapons; Complete military training  
Scout: Track 3 days; Survive wilderness week; Deliver crucial intel  
Thief: Pickpocket 20; Disarm 10 traps; Join thieves' guild  
Acolyte: 20 healing rituals; Divine vision; Convert 10 people  
Hedge Wizard: Cast 5 spells; Craft magic item; Survive magical mishap  
Merchant: 100 gold; 3 trade contacts; Complete major trade deal

Tier 2 to 3:  
Warrior: Win duel; Lead military op; Master 3 fighting styles  
Ranger: Solo dangerous beast; Map uncharted area; Bond with animal  
Rogue: Infiltrate guarded location; 500 gold heist; Establish informant network  
Priest: Public miracle; Establish temple; Defeat faith enemy  
Mage: Master 3 magic schools; Survive high Ether area; Decipher ancient text  
Bard: 1000 gold; Master instrument; Gather 5 cultural stories

Tier 3 to 4:  
Knight: Complete grand quest; Win tournament; Swear/uphold oath  
Hunter: Defeat legendary beast; Survive 5 extreme environments; Tame monster  
Assassin: Eliminate VIP; Create unique poison; Fake own death  
Cleric: Resurrect dead; Banish powerful entity; Receive divine mandate  
Sorcerer: 10 wild magic uses; Create new spell; Survive pure Ether contact  
Diplomat: Prevent war; Royal performance; Expose major conspiracy

Tier 4 to 5:  
Paladin: Lead campaign; Defeat legendary monster; Establish knight order  
Beast Master: Command legendary beasts; Thrive in deadliest environment; Solo defeat army with animals  
Shadowblade: Infiltrate secure fortress; Assassinate demon lord; Control throne from shadows  
High Priest: Become deity's voice; Banish god's avatar; Establish major religion  
Archmage: Control wild magic; Create major artifact; Establish magic school  
Loremaster: Broker nation peace; Uncover world-changing secret; Master 10 languages

Tier 5 to 6:  
Eternal Champion: Turn world war; Defeat Thar's avatar; Become greatest warrior  
Primal Hunter: Tame primordial beast; Shape-shift at will; Revive extinct species  
Shadow Walker: Perfect invisibility; Travel through shadows; Reshape continental politics unseen  
Avatar of the Gods: Ascend to demigod; Alter deity's domain; Win divine war  
Ether Lord: Reshape geography with magic; Create new plane; Achieve immortality  
World Shaper: Rewrite reality law; Uncover Aven/Thar's nature; Create new race
