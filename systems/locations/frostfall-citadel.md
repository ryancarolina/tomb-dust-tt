# Frostfall Citadel

Overview: Nestled atop the towering Frostspire Mountains, Frostfall Citadel is a fortress carved from ice and stone. It shimmers under the auroras that dance across the sky. This citadel serves as the domain of the Winter Court, ruled by the enigmatic Ice Sovereign.

Culture and Society: The inhabitants of Frostfall Citadel are adept at ice magic and survival in extreme cold. A strict hierarchy and a profound respect for the harsh beauty of winter mark their society. Celebrations and rituals revolve around the solstice, with ice sculptures and aurora-watching being key features.

Economy and Resources: The economy relies on mining precious stones and metals unique to the Frostspire Mountains and trading in ice-crafted artifacts known for their beauty and magical properties. The citadel is also a hub for alchemists seeking rare ingredients found only in the eternal frost.

Challenges and Threats: Beyond the constant threat of avalanches and blizzards, the citadel faces challenges from ice wraiths and frost giants that roam the mountains. The balance of power with the Winter Court is delicate, with political intrigue and elemental magic threatening to disrupt the icy peace.

Role in Aventhar: Frostfall Citadel stands as a testament to the endurance and majesty of winter. It is a place of magic, mystery, and political intrigue, offering adventurers unique challenges and the opportunity to engage with the powerful forces of ice and snow.
