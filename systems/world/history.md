# History of Aventhar

## Black Vale War (founding myth of the Knights)

A Thar cult breached the fen’s bedrock and opened the **Black Vale** rift. **Shadowkin** and **etherwraiths** poured out. **Ser Eirik Blackthorne** and ninety-eight companions held ninety-nine nights; on the last night the seal was laid—only Eirik survived. The **Knights of the Ninety-Ninth Night** maintain wards and hunt resurgent shadow. See [factions/knights-of-the-ninety-ninth-night.md](../factions/knights-of-the-ninety-ninth-night.md).

## The Sundering

Not a single day but a generation of storms, walking ruins, and failed god-rituals. The **Shadowfen capital** was abandoned in a week; Boydon Tower’s foundations cracked and were rebuilt higher. Scholars blame overlapping Ether wells; survivors blame both siblings’ silence.

## Ash Century

War between heartland kings and **Marcus Ironhand’s** unification drive forged the **Iron Pact**. Dwarves sealed undermountain vaults with **Ironbound Sentinels** still active. When the wars ended, thousands of trained fighters had no war—many became delvers.

## Age of Extraction (now)

Realms legalized **licensed delving** after plague and famine made salvage irresistible. The **Delver’s Registry** issues map claims, records deaths, and taxes recoveries. Breley licenses military delvers; Boydon licenses arcane probes; Edgecombe buries the rest.

**Player default:** ordinary people can die rich or die nameless; legends are deed-based (see [classes/progression.md](../classes/progression.md)), not prophesied.
