# The Ether

The **Ether** is the formless magical substance Aven and Thar used to weave Aventhar. It still permeates the world—thin everywhere, **thick** at thin-veils, battlefields, and god-tombs.

## How Ether behaves

| State | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Ambient** | Normal spells function; magical fauna |
| **Thick** | Advantage on spell attacks; wild surges on nat 1 (GM) |
| **Thin-veil** | Physical and Ether overlap; phasing creatures, wraiths, larvae |
| **Rift** | Black Vale–class catastrophe; requires seals and rituals |

## Signs of a thin-veil

- Colors “too vivid” at dusk  
- Echoes arrive before sound  
- Dead spellcasters’ hair floats  
- Compass spins near tomb mouths  

## Containment

- **Knights’ wards** on fen seals (Old Elandric inscriptions)  
- **Boydon** harmonic stabilizers near tower  
- **Ancient runes** repel [aetherial beasts](../monsters/aetherial-beasts.md)  
- **Salt circles** hinder [ether larvae](../monsters/ether-larva.md)  

## Creature link

| Ether phenomenon | Creatures |
|------------------|-----------|

| Fear and decay | Gloomspiders, etherwraiths |
| Storm primal | Sky serpents |
| Thar residue | Shadowkin, mirelings |
