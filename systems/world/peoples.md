# Peoples of Aventhar

Mechanical race rules: [character/races.md](../character/races.md). Lore per race: [races/](../races/).

## By region (common presence)

| Region | Dominant peoples | Notes |
|--------|------------------|-------|
| Heartlands | Humans, halflings, dwarves | Chivalric humans; halfling riverfolk |
| Whispering Marches | Humans, wood elves*, high elves | *Wood elves in character creation |
| Boydon / Skyreach | High elves, humans, gnomes | Scholars, monks |
| Shadowfen | Humans, dark elves, demonkin cultists | Mireling prey on all |
| Underdeep | Dwarves, dark elves | Iron Pact strongholds |
| Frostspire | Humans, dwarves | Frost-touched lineages |
| Coasts / deep water | Aquarids | Offshore enclaves |
| Plains | Centaurs, orcs | Nomadic bands; raid or trade |
| Verdant Vale | Mixed; druid-led | Species less important than oath |

## Attitudes toward delving

| People | View |
|--------|------|
| **Dwarves** | Delving is craft if licensed; hate Registry claim-jumpers in vaults |
| **High elves** | Prefer study to tomb-robbing; Boydon mediates |
| **Dark elves** | Salvage Thar-era relics; smuggle through fen |
| **Halflings** | Supply caravans; insure delvers jokingly |
| **Knights** | Delving acceptable if it weakens Black Vale remnants |
| **Druids (Vale)** | Desecration is crime |

## Languages (table convention)

- **Common** — trade tongue (Heartlands + Marches).  
- **Old Elandric** — wards, Knights’ rituals.  
- **Runic Dwarvish** — vaults, Iron Pact contracts.  
- **Sylvan** — Whispering Woods (Nature checks to parse).

NPCs and factions assume Common unless noted.
