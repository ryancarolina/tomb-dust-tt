# Marshal Garrick Holt

**Role:** Castellan of Breley’s outer ward; licenses military delvers and coordinates Knight patrols with Registry maps.

**Background:** Footman → Knight during Ash Century border wars. Lost a brother in **32-C-UG-1** (Breley undercrypt; hollow knights). Trusts deeds over birth.

**Appearance:** Square jaw, burn-scarred left hand, Breley blue tabard over practical plate.

**Personality:** Blunt, fair, superstitious about Eclipse week. Respects prepared parties; despises map-flippers who sell blind.
