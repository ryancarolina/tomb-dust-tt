# Etherwraiths

## Lore

**Origin:** Souls and memories shredded at Ether thin-places—especially the Black Vale rift wars remembered by the Knights of the Ninety-Ninth Night. They are not true undead but **echoes** that hunger for warmth and identity.

**Description:** Human-shaped voids with faces that shift between victims the party has lost. Cold touch; whisper in languages the listener almost understands.

**Abilities (lore):** Pass through living flesh leaving frost; **possess** the weak-willed briefly; **feed** on spellcasting.

**Weaknesses:** **Aven’s light** (consecrated ground); **speaking a victim’s true name** forces the wraith to show its hollow core (disadvantage on attacks for 1 round).
