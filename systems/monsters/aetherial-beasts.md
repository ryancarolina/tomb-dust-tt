# Aetherial Beasts

## Lore

**Origin:** Born where the Ether is concentrated—ancient battlegrounds, fallen star sites, ruins where the veil is thin.

**Description:** Amorphous, nightmare-shaped forms that shimmer with Ether-light. They phase through stone or solidify into biting, hungry mass.

**Abilities (lore):** **Phasing** through matter; **energy absorption** from spells and enchantments (heals or empowers them).

**Weaknesses:** **Ether overload** (bait extra absorption → unstable burst); **ancient runes** repel or contain them.
