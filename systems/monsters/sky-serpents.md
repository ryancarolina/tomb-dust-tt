# Sky Serpents

## Lore

**Origin:** Ether and primal storm; seen before tempests as weather-heralds.

**Description:** Dragon-long, wingless serpents; scales like storm clouds; eyes crackle with lightning.

**Abilities (lore):** **Storm calling** and **wind control**.

**Weaknesses:** **Sound vibrations** (specific harmonics); **ground rare minerals** that damp storm magic.
