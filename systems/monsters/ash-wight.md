# Ash Wight

## Lore

**Origin:** Victims of magical fires and volcanic vents near Skyreach; ash binds their spirit to scorched bone.

**Description:** Char-black skeleton wreathed in embers; leaves soot footprints and cold heat.

**Weaknesses:** Water douses aura (disadvantage on attacks until dry); holy water.
