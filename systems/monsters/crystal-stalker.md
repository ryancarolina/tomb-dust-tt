# Crystal Stalker

## Lore

**Origin:** Predators crystallized in the Crystaline Caverns—living refractions of the petrified forest legend.

**Description:** Quadruped panther shape made of faceted quartz; movement chimes softly until it pounces.

**Weaknesses:** Thunderous impact (shatter risk); bludgeoning from behind (disadvantage on AC vs called shots, GM).
