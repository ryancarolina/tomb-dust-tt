# Fen Stalker

## Lore

**Origin:** Natural apex predator of Shadowfen and Mireling-adjacent bogs—not a shapeshifter, but a crocodilian horror that **hunts** mimics’ leftovers.

**Description:** Low, armored body; eyes at waterline; breath smells of rot and copper.

**Weaknesses:** Loud noise on shore (disadvantage on Stealth there); harpoons and salt-lined nets.
