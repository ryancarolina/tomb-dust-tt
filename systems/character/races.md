## Creating a character and determining base scores {#creating-a-character-and-determining-base-scores}

Playable Races \- select a race\!

1\. **Humans**  
   \- Attribute Adjustments: \+1 to any two attributes of the player's choice.  
   \- Description: Adaptable and versatile, humans thrive in various environments and possess a remarkable capacity for learning and mastering multiple disciplines.

2\. **High Elves**  
   \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 INT, \+1 AGI  
   \- Description: Graceful and wise, high elves live in harmony with the natural magic of Aventhar, excelling in arcane arts and knowledge.

3\. **Dark Elves**  
   \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 AGI, \+1 INT, \-1 SPI  
   \- Description: Embracing the teachings of Thar, dark elves reside in the underworld's depths, mastering dark magic and craftsmanship.

4\. **Wood Elves**  
   \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 AGI, \+1 SPI  
   \- Description: Masters of the forests, wood elves are agile and deeply connected to nature, excelling in archery and natural magic.

5\. **Dwarves**  
   \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 STA, \+1 STR, \-1 AGI  
   \- Description: Masters of craftsmanship and mining, dwarves are known for their resilience, honor, and loyalty, defending their deep mountain homes.

6\. **Halflings**  
   \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 AGI, \+1 SPI  
   \- Description: Halflings are small but resilient, living in harmony with nature and embodying the essence of contentment and courage.

7\. **Centaurs**  
   \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 STR, \+1 AGI, \-1 INT  
   \- Description: Roaming the plains and forests, centaurs are protectors of nature, renowned for their speed, strength, and wisdom.

8\. **Aquarids**  
   \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 AGI, \+1 SPI, \-1 STA  
   \- Description: Humanoid beings from the deep seas, Aquarids excel in swimming, communication with sea creatures, and water manipulation.

9\. **Demonkin**  
   \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 SPI, \+1 STR, \-1 AGI  
   \- Description: Born from Thar's essence, demonkin are adept in dark magic, often driven by the desire to extend their influence and escape their infernal masters.

10\. **Orcs**  
    \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 STR, \+1 STA, \-1 INT  
    \- Description: Strong and fierce warriors from rugged lands, orcs excel in combat and survival, embodying brute strength and resilience.

11\. **Gnomes**  
    \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 INT, \+1 AGI, \-1 STR  
    \- Description: Inventive and quick-witted, gnomes are known for their ingenuity and dexterity, often excelling in technology and arcane studies.

12\. **Dragonkin**  
    \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 STR, \+1 SPI, \-1 AGI  
    \- Description: Bearing the blood of dragons, dragonkin are powerful and resilient, with a natural affinity for elemental magic.

13\. **Faeries**  
    \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 AGI, \+1 INT, \-1 STR  
    \- Description: Small and magical, faeries are known for their agility and trickery, often using their magical abilities to outwit larger foes.

14\. **Minotaurs**  
    \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 STR, \+1 STA, \-1 INT  
    \- Description: Powerful and imposing, minotaurs are known for their strength and endurance, often excelling as warriors and protectors.

15\. **Undead**  
    \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 SPI, \+1 STA, \-1 AGI  
    \- Description: Raised from the dead, undead are resilient to physical damage and possess a strong will, often excelling in necromantic arts.

16\. **Trolls**  
    \- Attribute Adjustments: \+2 STR, \+1 AGI, \-1 INT  
    \- Description: Tall and strong, trolls are known for their regenerative abilities and combat prowess, often excelling as fierce warriors and hunters.
