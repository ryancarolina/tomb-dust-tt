# Knights of the Ninety-Ninth Night

Origins:  
The Knights of the Ninety-Ninth Night trace their origins to **Elandria**—the ancient name for the **Ealdormere** heartland (see [world/aventhar-overview.md](../world/aventhar-overview.md)). Founded during a time of great turmoil, the faction was established by a legendary knight named Ser Eirik Blackthorne. According to legend, Ser Eirik and ninety-eight other warriors fought tirelessly for ninety-nine nights to defend Elandria from a relentless horde of shadowy creatures that emerged from the depths of the Black Vale. On the ninety-ninth night, they finally succeeded in sealing the rift from which the creatures emerged, but at great cost. Only Ser Eirik survived, and he vowed to create a brotherhood dedicated to preventing such dark forces from ever threatening Elandria again. The Knights are devout followers of Aven, the deity of light and protection, whom they believe guided Ser Eirik in his battle against the shadows.
Goals:  
Guardianship Against Darkness: To guard Elandria against the resurgence of dark forces and shadowy creatures. Preservation of Knowledge: To preserve ancient knowledge and artifacts that could be used to combat or prevent dark magic. Training and Recruitment: To train new generations of warriors and scholars to join their ranks and continue their mission. Alliance Building: To forge alliances with other factions and kingdoms to create a united front against common threats.
Activities:  
Patrols and Expeditions: Regularly patrolling known areas of dark activity and organizing expeditions to explore and secure ancient ruins, tombs, and other places of power. Rituals and Ceremonies: Performing ancient rituals to maintain protective wards and seals on places where dark forces might emerge. Training Camps: Operating training camps where recruits are trained in combat, strategy, and the arcane arts. Artifact Retrieval: Seeking out and retrieving powerful artifacts that could be used to fight against dark forces or that must be kept out of the hands of evil. Research and Study: Maintaining libraries and research centers dedicated to studying ancient texts, dark magic, and the history of Elandria.
Challenges:  
Internal Strife: Maintaining unity within the faction can be challenging, especially with differing opinions on how best to achieve their goals. External Threats: They face constant threats from dark forces, rival factions, and rogue sorcerers who seek to unleash chaos. Resource Limitations: The Knights often struggle with limited resources, making it difficult to maintain their extensive operations and training programs. Political Intrigue: Navigating the complex political landscape of Elandria and securing alliances can be fraught with danger and betrayal. Preservation of Secrets: Balancing the need to preserve ancient knowledge with the risk of such knowledge falling into the wrong hands is a constant concern.
**Headquarters:** Ward-chapel at Breley Keep; fen patrols from Shadowfen Causeway forts.

Role in Aventhar:  
Protectors: The Knights of the Ninety-Ninth Night are seen as protectors of Ealdormere (Elandria), guarding against dark forces and preventing the resurgence of ancient evils. Advisors: They often serve as advisors to rulers and leaders, providing counsel on matters related to magic, ancient history, and defense. Keepers of Knowledge: The faction is respected for its extensive libraries and research centers, and scholars from across Elandria seek their expertise. Trainers of Heroes: The Knights are known for their rigorous training programs, and many of Elandria's greatest warriors and mages have passed through their ranks. Symbols of Hope: The Knights represent hope and resilience to the people of Elandria, embodying the belief that the forces of good can prevail against darkness.
