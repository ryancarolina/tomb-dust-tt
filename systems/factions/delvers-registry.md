# Delver's Registry

**Origins:** Founded mid–Ash Century when war salvage outpaced law. Merchants, ex-soldiers, and tower scribes formed a **neutral guild** to stamp map claims, record deaths, and tax recovery so realms would not hang every scavenger for grave-robbing.

**Goals:**  
- Standardize **licensed delving** (map stamps, insurance pools).  
- Prevent claim wars from sparking another continental war.  
- Publish **survivor rolls** and blacklist fraud maps.

**Activities:**  
- **Registry Moots** (monthly): auctions, dispute courts, hiring boards.  
- **Clerks** in Edgecombe, Breley lower town, Boydon’s lake dock.  
- **Survey contracts** for unmapped thin-veils and ruin wings.  
- **Ash tithe:** small cut of all sales funds burial of unclaimed Tomb Dust.

**Challenges:**  
- **Iron Pact** wants vault jurisdiction; Registry wants profit share.  
- **Knights** demand veto on Black Vale–adjacent maps.  
- **Verdant Vale** sabotages grotto listings.  
- Forged stamps (wrong **AV-GRID** or fake `UG` levels, especially **47-B** fen charts) ruin trust.

**Role in Aventhar:** The Registry is how ordinary people enter the extraction economy. Without it, delving is outlaw banditry; with it, death is still likely—but **documented**. PCs usually start in debt for gear or a map stamp.

**Key NPC:** [Mira Ashret](../npcs/mira-ashret.md) (Edgecombe clerk).

## Claims and insurance (TD-053)

| Service | Rule |
|---------|------|
| **Map stamp** | Buys 30-day **on-claim** rights for one **AV-GRID** address + layer stack |
| **Off-claim** | Wrong cell/layer/expired stamp → no insurance, legal risk ([world/extraction.md](../world/extraction.md)) |
| **Insurance pool** | Optional premium (10–30 gp by tier); pays **50 gp + one item** to account on stamped on-claim death |
| **Disputes** | **Registry Moot** — monthly court; claim-jump, forgery, fence fraud |
| **Forgery** | Wrong `UG` level on chart → stamp void; fine equal to stamp cost + rep −1 |
