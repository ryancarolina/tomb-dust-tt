# Iron Pact

Origins:  
Formed by disgruntled adventurers and ex-soldiers seeking order in the chaotic lands, the Iron Pact emerged as a response to the lawlessness that plagued the regions. These individuals, having experienced the disarray and unpredictability of life as adventurers and soldiers, banded together with the shared vision of imposing a new order. Led by a charismatic former general named Marcus Ironhand, they vowed to create a society based on strength, discipline, and unwavering authority.
Goals:  
To establish a new world order through strength and discipline. The Iron Pact seeks to bring stability to the lands by imposing strict laws and a rigid hierarchical structure. They believe that only through ironclad rules and powerful leadership can true peace and prosperity be achieved.
Activities:  
Militaristic training camps are the backbone of the Iron Pact, where recruits undergo rigorous physical and mental training to become disciplined soldiers. The faction enforces strict laws within their territories, ensuring order and compliance. They also pursue expansionist policies, aiming to extend their control over more regions through both diplomacy and conquest.
Challenges:  
The Iron Pact frequently clashes with other factions that oppose their authoritarian methods and expansionist ambitions. Internal power struggles also pose a significant challenge, as ambitious leaders within the faction vie for control and influence. Maintaining unity and preventing dissent among their ranks is a constant struggle.
Role in Aventhar:  
The Iron Pact is a controversial force that brings both stability and conflict to the world of Aventhar. While they provide a sense of order and security in areas plagued by chaos, their authoritarian approach often leads to tensions with other factions and local populations. Some view them as saviors who bring much-needed discipline, while others see them as oppressors who stifle freedom and autonomy. Their influence spans multiple regions, including **Ealdormere** and the **Underdeep**, making them a significant power in Aventhar's political and social landscape. They contest **Registry** claims in dwarven vaults ([delvers-registry.md](delvers-registry.md)).
