# Dance with the Dead

**When:** Winter solstice (between Eclipse Festivals).  
**Where:** Registry towns (Edgecombe largest), Breley crypt yards, Boydon memorial cloisters.

## Description

Not a celebration of death—a **reckoning** with it. Delvers who survived the year carry ash pouches (their own near-misses, or Tomb Dust from friends) and dance a slow circle while names are read aloud. Aven’s candles and Thar’s cold bells alternate each verse.

## Activities

- **Name reading:** Registry clerk calls every delver lost on stamped maps that year; survivors respond “Still walking.”  
- **Ash spiral:** collective scatter at crossroads (believed to confuse hungry wraiths).  
- **Quiet bargains:** fences and knights hire cheap for next season’s maps.  
- **Prohibition:** no drawn weapons in the circle; breaking it is bad luck (GM: Fortune pool −1 for offenders).

## Significance

Binds the extraction economy to community. Even Iron Pact soldiers attend in neutral territory. Verdant Vale sends observers—not participants—unless a grove-tomb was desecrated (then druids demand restitution).

## Challenges (adventure hooks)

- **Grave-ghoul** attracted to ash concentration.  
- **Etherwraith** mimics a name during reading (Insight DC 14).  
- **Map auction** afterward turns violent (rival claims on **47-B-UG-3**).  
- **Thar cult** uses dance to recruit grieving kin.

## Relation to Eclipse Festival

Eclipse is public unity; Dance is **delver-private grief**. Factions truce during Eclipse week but negotiate hard the night after Dance.
