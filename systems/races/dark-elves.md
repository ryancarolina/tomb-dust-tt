# Dark Elves

Origins: The Dark Elves have embraced Thar's teachings, finding sanctuary in the underworld's depths and shunning the surface's light.

Culture & Society: Their society is marked by a fierce survival ethic. They worship deities that embody Aventhar's darker aspects and value power and sacrifice.

Abilities: They are experts in dark magic and craftsmanship. They forge alliances with subterranean creatures and excel at creating artifacts with shadowy powers.

Role in Aventhar: As outcasts, they represent the world's capacity for darkness and resilience, surviving where others cannot.

Challenges: Their rejection by surface dwellers fuels a deep-seated animosity, leading to conflicts that test their strength and cunning.
