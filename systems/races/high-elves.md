# High Elves

Origins: Deeply connected to the Ether, High Elves embody grace and wisdom and live in harmony with Aventhar's natural magic.

Culture & Society: Their cities are centers of learning and artistic expression, dedicated to preserving the balance between the physical and magical realms.

Abilities: They have an innate connection to magic and wield it with finesse. They use their abilities to protect the realm and guide its inhabitants toward harmony.

Role in Aventhar: High Elves serve as custodians of knowledge and natural beauty, reminding all of Aventhar's inherent magic and the importance of maintaining balance.

Challenges: Their longevity and power sometimes isolate them from other races, and their dedication to balance makes them targets for those who seek to upset it.
