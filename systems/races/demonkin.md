# Demonkin

Origins: Born from Thar's destructive essence, Demonkind inhabits the infernal regions of Aventhar, thriving on chaos and strife.

Culture & Society: Organized under a power hierarchy, with Demon Lords leading through fear and manipulation, they are driven to extend their influence and spread corruption. Though some demonkin have been known to simply want to escape the chains of their masters and seek far different lives in the form of adventuring.

Abilities: Demonkind is adept in dark magic, capable of bending wills and mastering the arts of deception and corruption.

Role in Aventhar: They embody the world's chaotic and destructive aspects, challenging the forces of good and testing the resolve of Aventhar's defenders.

Challenges: Their ambition often leads to internal strife and conflicts with Aventhar's other races as they seek to dominate rather than coexist.
