# Aven

The Beacon of Creation and Complexities
Domain: Aven embodies creation, order, engineering, and the relentless pursuit of excellence in life's endeavors. As the sun brings light to Aventhar, Aven symbolizes the warmth of passion and the illumination of innovation.

Appearance and Symbols: Aven is depicted with a sunburst halo, signifying his domain over creation and the clarity of day. Symbols associated with Aven include an anvil and hammer for engineering and creation, a family crest representing devotion to kin, and a ship's anchor reflecting his naval service and the stability he strives to provide.

Personality and Virtues: Aven is driven, passionate, and fiercely protective of his creations and those under his care. His engineer's mind sees the world in systems and structures, constantly seeking to build and improve. However, Aven's passion sometimes manifests as a fiery temper, and his commitment to his family and ambitions can isolate him from broader circles. His military background and interest in martial arts reflect a readiness to confront challenges head-on, both physically and metaphorically.

Flaws and Challenges: Aven struggles with anger management and balancing his ego and responsibilities. His pursuit of new ventures and appreciation for the female form can sometimes distract him from the path of creation and order. These human elements make him a relatable deity who understands his followers' struggles.

## Worship in Aventhar

**Sunrise rites** at Breley; engineers and knights claim Aven. **Eclipse Festival** honors his unity with Thar. **Wards** against shadow use Old Elandric prayers to Aven. Delvers light a candle before first descent (Fortune tradition, not required).

**Opposed:** Thar cults, Black Vale rift, desecration without license.
