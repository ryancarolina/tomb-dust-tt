# Thar

The Guardian of Transformation and Turbulence
Domain: Thar governs over destruction, chaos, and the transformative power of adversity. As the moon that oversees the night, Thar represents the unseen, the unexplored, and the potential within chaos to give birth to new forms of existence.

Appearance and Symbols: Thar is depicted as a figure cloaked in shadows, with a crescent moon hovering above. His symbols include a broken chain, representing liberation from the old to make way for the new; a two-faced coin, symbolizing the dual nature of chaos and transformation; and an open road, reflecting his final journey and the uncharted paths he encourages his followers to tread.

Personality and Virtues: Thar is enigmatic, embodying the capriciousness of change and the depth of the unknown. His domain over destruction is not mindless havoc but the necessary end to stagnation and the precursor to creation. He champions adaptability, resilience, and the strength to navigate life's unpredictabilities.

Flaws and Challenges: Thar's relationship with Aven is complex, marked by moments of contention and cooperation. Their sibling rivalry and differing natures often lead to conflict but also balance. Thar's untimely death and the memories of shared adventures through games and storytelling infuse him with a poignant legacy that teaches the importance of reconciliation and the value of shared experiences amidst differences.

## Worship in Aventhar

**Moonrise** cults in fen and underdeep; **honest chaos** (change, not senseless murder). **Dance with the Dead** rings Thar’s bell after Aven’s candle. Dark elves honor Thar as teacher of transformation—not the Black Vale rift, which they blame on broken seals.

**Opposed:** Knights’ wards, uncontrolled rifts, cults that seek annihilation.
