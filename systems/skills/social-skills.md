# Social Skills

All checks: **d20 + ability mod + PB + skill bonus** vs DC (or vs target's opposing check). Skill bonus from [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md).

---

## 1. Persuasion (SPI)

**Check:** d20 + SPI mod + PB + Persuasion skill bonus vs target's Willpower DC (typically 10 + SPI mod + PB)

**Skill level effects:** Improve attitude one step per 3 skill levels on a strong success.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Silver Tongue:** Reroll one failed Persuasion check per day.
- **Level 6 — Compelling Argument:** Target has disadvantage on Willpower saves vs your Persuasion.
- **Level 9 — Mass Appeal:** Affect a group of up to (skill level) individuals with one check at −2.

---

## 2. Intimidation (STR or SPI, higher mod)

**Check:** d20 + higher of STR or SPI mod + PB + Intimidation skill bonus vs Willpower DC

**Skill level effects:** Successful intimidation lasts 1 round per skill level.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Frightening Presence:** Intimidate all enemies within 30 ft as one action.
- **Level 6 — Breaking Point:** On a success by 5+, target becomes compliant for 1d4 hours out of fear.
- **Level 9 — Terrifying Aura:** Enemies within 10 ft have disadvantage on attacks against you.

---

## 3. Deception (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Deception skill bonus vs target's Insight

**Skill level effects:** Maintain lies with (skill level) complex elements.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Poker Face:** Advantage to maintain a lie under scrutiny.
- **Level 6 — False Identity:** Persistent alter ego: +3 to related Deception checks.
- **Level 9 — Grand Deception:** Once per week, automatically succeed on one Deception check.

---

## 4. Etiquette (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Etiquette skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** Appear one social rank higher per 3 skill levels (temporary).

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Social Chameleon:** Advantage in unfamiliar cultural settings.
- **Level 6 — Faux Pas Aversion:** Once per day, reroll a failed Etiquette check.
- **Level 9 — Perfect Host/Guest:** Gain a significant NPC contact after a major social event success.

---

## 5. Leadership (SPI)

**Check:** d20 + SPI mod + PB + Leadership skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** Lead groups of up to (skill level × 5) effectively.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Rally:** Once per day, allies within 30 ft gain +1 on checks for 1d4 rounds.
- **Level 6 — Inspire Greatness:** Grant one ally advantage on their next check as a bonus action.
- **Level 9 — Legendary Commander:** Once per week, automatically succeed on one Leadership check for a large group.

---

## 6. Insight (SPI)

**Check:** d20 + SPI mod + PB + Insight skill bonus vs target's Deception

**Skill level effects:** Detect one subtle tell per 2 skill levels on success.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Gut Feeling:** Once per day, advantage on one Insight check.
- **Level 6 — Cold Read:** One Insight check as a free action per social encounter.
- **Level 9 — Mind's Eye:** Once per day, know if a creature within 30 ft is lying without rolling.
