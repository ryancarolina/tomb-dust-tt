# Subterfuge Skills

All checks: **d20 + ability mod + PB + skill bonus** vs DC (or vs opposing check). Skill bonus from [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md).

---

## 1. Lockpicking (AGI)

**Check:** d20 + AGI mod + PB + Lockpicking skill bonus vs Lock DC

**Skill level effects:** Reduce pick time by 10% per level.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Quick Pick:** Attempt a simple lock as a bonus action.
- **Level 6 — Lock Whisperer:** Advantage vs mechanical traps on locks.
- **Level 9 — Master Unlocking:** Once per day, automatically open one non-magical lock.

---

## 2. Trap Handling (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Trap Handling skill bonus vs Trap DC

**Skill level effects:** Handle traps of complexity up to skill level.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Trap Sense:** Advantage on saves vs traps.
- **Level 6 — Quick Disarm:** Disarm a trap in half the normal time.
- **Level 9 — Trapmaster:** Traps you create are +2 DC to detect and disarm.

---

## 3. Forgery (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Forgery skill bonus vs inspector's Insight

**Skill level effects:** Fools inspectors with Insight up to (skill level + 5) on a success.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Quick Sketch:** Simple forgery in half the normal time.
- **Level 6 — Master Forger:** Advantage to create or detect forgeries.
- **Level 9 — Perfect Fake:** Once per week, create a forgery that resists normal detection (Insight vs DC 20+).

---

## 4. Disguise (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Disguise skill bonus vs observer's Perception

**Skill level effects:** Maintain a disguise for (skill level) hours without touch-ups.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Quick Change:** Don or remove a prepared disguise as a bonus action.
- **Level 6 — Method Actor:** Advantage on social checks while in disguise.
- **Level 9 — Face Dancer:** Once per day, mimic someone studied for at least 1 hour.

---

## 5. Appraisal (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Appraisal skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** Estimate value within (20% − 2% per level) of true value.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Quick Appraisal:** Roughly appraise an item as a bonus action.
- **Level 6 — Eye for Quality:** Advantage to detect fakes or inferior goods.
- **Level 9 — Master Appraiser:** Once per day, perfectly determine value and properties.

---

## 6. Eavesdropping (SPI)

**Check:** d20 + SPI mod + PB + Eavesdropping skill bonus vs speaker's Stealth

**Skill level effects:** Clear listening at (10 + 5 × skill level) feet.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Lip Reading:** Understand speech by sight within 30 ft.
- **Level 6 — Selective Hearing:** Filter background noise for clarity (advantage in noisy environments).
- **Level 9 — Clairaudience:** Once per day, listen to a conversation up to 1 mile away for 1 minute (SPI save vs ward DC if magical).
