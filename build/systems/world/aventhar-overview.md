# Aventhar — Overview

**Aventhar** is the known world: one great continent and shattered offshore reaches, bound by the **Silversea** to the west and the **Frostspire** chain to the north. Campaign play defaults to the **central belt**—from Ealdormere’s hills through the Whispering Marches into Shadowfen and up to Skyreach.

## Regions

### Heartlands (Ealdormere)

Rolling farmland, river trade, and **Breley Keep**—Aven’s martial ideal made stone. The realm’s modern name is **Ealdormere**; chronicles call the same heartland **Elandria** before the Sundering. Law, knighthood, and engineered fortifications dominate.

### Whispering Marches

Border woods, lake traffic, and **Boydon Tower**. Ether bleeds from the **Whispering Woods**; villages like **Edgecombe** live on herbs, guides, and delver traffic. Highest concentration of thin-veil incidents outside tomb-fields.

### Shadowfen

Marsh, ruin, and perpetual mist. Pre-Sundering city drowned in politics and Thar-cult uprising; now **Mirelings**, **fen stalkers**, and **etherwraiths** thrive. Black Vale rift (sealed by the Ninety-Ninth Night) lies beneath the deepest fen.

### Skyreach

Storm cliffs and **Skyreach Monastery** (stars and Ether). **Sky serpents** nest in thermals; mineral dust from the cliffs dampens their storm-calling. Trade: altitude herbs, lightning glass.

### Frostspire

**Frostfall Citadel** and the **Winter Court**—ice nobility, aurora ritual, and things that walk in whiteout. Politically distant but sends envoys to Eclipse Festival.

### Verdant Vale

Hidden valley; druid consensus rule. Barter economy; refuses Registry salvage from desecrated groves.

### Underdeep

**Crystaline Caverns** and dwarven holds tied to the **Iron Pact**. Crystal magic, stalkers, and vault-sentinel remnants.

## Trade routes

1. **King’s Road** — Breley ↔ inland towns ↔ Edgecombe (delver supplies).  
2. **Silverlake Run** — Boydon ↔ Edgecombe by barge (arcane freight).  
3. **Fen Causeway** — risky; Registry-paid escorts to ruin markers.  
4. **High Pass** — Skyreach ↔ Frostfall (seasonal; closed in deep winter).

## AV-GRID (locations)

Every site has a coordinate: **column-row** on the surface (`23-A` = Boydon Tower), plus layers for depth and other planes (`23-A-UG-1` = undercroft, `47-B-BV` = Black Vale rift). See **[grid.md](grid.md)** and **[cell-atlas.md](cell-atlas.md)**.
