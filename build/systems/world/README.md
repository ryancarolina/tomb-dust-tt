## Cosmology (quick)

**Aven** (sun) and **Thar** (moon) created **Aventhar** and infused it with the **Ether**. They withdrew to watch; magic and catastrophe both flow from Ether concentration. See [lore/aventhar-creation.md](../lore/aventhar-creation.md).

## Present era (play default)

The **Age of Extraction**: sealed tombs, thin-veils, and ruin-fields draw delvers who sell maps, salvage, and glory—knowing most return as ash or **Tomb Dust**. Realms tolerate the trade because it funds war, craft, and spell research while bleeding the reckless.
