# Weapons

Weapon attacks use **d20 + ability mod + PB + weapon skill bonus** vs **AC**. Damage is **weapon die + ability mod + skill bonus** (see [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md) and [skills/combat-skills.md](../skills/combat-skills.md)).

**Ability column:** which attribute applies to attack and damage by default. **Finesse** weapons may use STR or AGI (higher modifier).

## Property glossary

| Property | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Finesse** | Use STR or AGI mod (whichever is higher) for attack and damage |
| **Light** | Eligible for off-hand / dual-wield rules |
| **Two-hand** | Requires two hands to attack |
| **Versatile** | One hand (listed die); two hands (+1 die step, shown in notes) |
| **Reach** | May attack targets **2 squares** away (see Polearm Proficiency) |
| **Loading** | Cannot attack again until reloaded (see weapon notes) |
| **Thrown** | Ranged attack using thrown range; uses Throwing Weapons skill |
| **Ranged** | Ranged attack using Archery or Crossbow Expertise as noted |

**Crossbow reload:** After firing a crossbow, you must spend **1 action** to reload before you can fire again. Crossbow Expertise techniques may reduce reload cost (minimum bonus action).

**Thrown / ranged ranges:** Short / long distance in feet. Attacks beyond short range have disadvantage; beyond long range cannot be attempted.

|--------|------|--------|------|---------|------------|
| Dagger | 2gp | 1d4 | Piercing | AGI | Finesse, light, thrown 20/60 |
| Club | 1gp | 1d4 | Bludgeoning | STR | Light |
| Quarterstaff | 2gp | 1d6 | Bludgeoning | STR | Two-hand, versatile (1d8) |
| Sling | 1gp | 1d4 | Bludgeoning | AGI | Ranged 60/240, loading |
| Thrown rock | — | 1d4 | Bludgeoning | STR | Thrown 20/60 |

*Thrown rock:* improvised; no cost; GM may impose disadvantage on attack rolls.
