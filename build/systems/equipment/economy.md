## Starting Gold Calculation

Starting GP \= (Base Class GP \+ Life Event Modifier) x 1d6

1\. Base Class GP:  
Peasant: 10gp | Laborer: 15gp | Urchin: 5gp  
Apprentice: 20gp | Militia: 25gp | Novice: 15gp

2\. Life Event Modifier:  
Severe Illness: \-5gp | Grueling Labor: \+5gp | Athletic Training: \+0gp  
Intense Study: \+10gp | Mystical Encounter: \+15gp | Childhood Trauma: \-10gp  
Adventurous Upbringing: \+5gp | Scholarly Pursuits: \+15gp | Peaceful Life: \+0gp  
Isolated Childhood: \-5gp | Military Training: \+10gp | Magical Awakening: \+20gp  
Parental Loss: \-5gp | Orphaned Young: \-10gp | Hard Labor: \+5gp  
Blessed with Health: \+5gp | Nimble Childhood: \+0gp | Farm Life: \+5gp  
Severe Injury: \-10gp | Early Mentorship: \+10gp | Harsh Environment: \-5gp  
Frequent Travel: \+5gp | Sickness Recovery: \+0gp | Rich Education: \+25gp  
Desert Life: \-5gp | Lonely Upbringing: \-5gp | Combat Training: \+10gp  
Regular Meditation: \+5gp | Nomadic Life: \+0gp | Repeated Trauma: \-15gp  
Street Smarts: \+5gp | High Society: \+30gp | Religious Upbringing: \+10gp  
War Survivor: \+0gp | Jungle Life: \-5gp | Early Loss of Family: \-10gp  
Forest Dweller: \+0gp | Wealthy Childhood: \+35gp | Harsh Winters: \-5gp

3\. Multiplier: Roll 1d6

Example: Militia (25gp) with Farm Life (+5gp) rolls 4 on 1d6  
Calculation: (25 \+ 5\) x 4 \= 120gp starting gold

Note: Minimum starting gold is 0gp (if calculation results in a negative number).
