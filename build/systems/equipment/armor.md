# Armor and shields

Armor adds to **Armor Class (AC)**. See [core/derived-stats.md](../core/derived-stats.md) and [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md).

**AC = 10 + armor AC + agility bonus (capped by category) + shield + Dodge (if alert) + misc**

## Armor categories

| Category | AGI to AC | Movement penalty |
|----------|-----------|------------------|
| None / clothes | Full AGI mod | 0 |
| Light | Full AGI mod | 0 |
| Medium | AGI mod capped at **+2** | −2 (min move 2) |
| Heavy | **No** AGI mod | −4 (min move 2) |

**Heavy armor:** disadvantage on Stealth; disadvantage on Acrobatics while worn.  
**Medium armor:** optional −1 to Stealth (GM).

## Shields

Requires one hand. Add to AC when equipped and you are aware of the attacker.

| Shield | Cost | AC bonus |
|--------|------|----------|
| Buckler | 5gp | +1 |
| Shield (kite) | 10gp | +2 |
| Tower shield | 30gp | +3 |

Tower shield: −1 to Stealth; GM may limit AGI bonus to +1 when using one.

## Knight vs agile fighter (examples)

**Knight (alert):** plate +8, shield +2 → AC **20** before misc; slow movement; keeps AC when flat-footed.

**Ninja (alert):** studded leather +3, AGI +4, Dodge +2 → AC **19**; full movement; **flat-footed** drops to AC **13** (10 + 3 only).
