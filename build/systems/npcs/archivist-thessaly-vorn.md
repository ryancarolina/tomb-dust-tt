# Archivist Thessaly Vorn

**Role:** Third-circle archivist, **Boydon Tower**; catalogs Ether anomalies and delver reports from the Marches.

**Background:** Apprentice → Mage path; specializes in storm-ether interactions with [sky-serpents](../monsters/sky-serpents.md). Employs Lyra as external consultant.

**Appearance:** Spectacles etched with warding runes; storm-gray robes; copper astrolabe.

**Personality:** Curious to a fault; will trade tower access for rare samples. Fears Thar-cult texts but studies them anyway.
