# Mira Ashret

**Role:** Senior clerk, **Delver's Registry** (Edgecombe satellite office).

**Background:** Urchin → Thief → legitimate clerk after surviving Whispering Woods map. Keeps a ledger of every name she buried from Tomb Dust runs.

**Appearance:** Ink-stained fingers, short hair, Registry brass stamp on a chain.

**Personality:** Warm to survivors, ice-cold to map forgers. Speaks Common and pidgin Old Elandric for ward contracts.
