# Lyra the Stormcaller

**Background:** Powerful mage from Boydon Tower; commands storms and teaches weather magic.

**Role:** Protects the land from natural disasters; mentor to apprentices.

**Appearance:** Tall; robes like storm clouds; eyes spark with lightning.

**Personality:** Stern but fair; deep duty to protect the realm.
