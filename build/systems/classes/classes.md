## Stat requirements, abilities, and roles for each class {#stat-requirements,-abilities,-and-roles-for-each-class}

Tier 1: Base Classes

At creation, **Base HP** is **10** for all Tier-1 classes; **Base MP** varies by class (see [core/derived-stats.md](../core/derived-stats.md)).

1\. Peasant  
   \- Stat Requirements: None  
   \- Base MP: 5  
   \- Key Abilities: Basic farming, animal handling, simple crafts  
   \- Role in Party: Jack-of-all-trades, support  
   \- Place in World: Common folk, backbone of society

2\. Laborer  
   \- Stat Requirements: STR 8+  
   \- Key Abilities: Heavy lifting, endurance, basic construction  
   \- Role in Party: Pack mule, physical tasks  
   \- Place in World: Construction workers, dock workers, manual laborers

3\. Urchin  
   \- Stat Requirements: AGI 8+  
   \- Key Abilities: Stealth, pickpocketing, street smarts  
   \- Role in Party: Scout, thief  
   \- Place in World: Street kids, beggars, petty thieves

4\. Apprentice  
   \- Stat Requirements: INT 8+  
   \- Key Abilities: Basic literacy, simple spells, craft basics  
   \- Role in Party: Lore, basic magical support  
   \- Place in World: Students, assistants to craftsmen or mages

5\. Militia  
   \- Stat Requirements: STR 8+ or AGI 8+  
   \- Key Abilities: Basic combat, patrol, local knowledge  
   \- Role in Party: Basic fighter, guide  
   \- Place in World: Town guards, local defenders

6\. Novice  
   \- Stat Requirements: SPI 8+  
   \- Key Abilities: Basic healing, simple blessings, religious knowledge  
   \- Role in Party: Healer, spiritual advisor  
   \- Place in World: Acolytes, initiates in religious orders

Tier 2: Experienced Classes

1\. Footman  
   \- Stat Requirements: STR 10+, STA 10+  
   \- Key Abilities: Melee combat, shield use, battle tactics  
   \- Role in Party: Front-line fighter, defender  
   \- Place in World: Professional soldiers, guards in wealthy establishments

2\. Scout  
   \- Stat Requirements: AGI 10+, INT 10+  
   \- Key Abilities: Tracking, survival, ranged combat  
   \- Role in Party: Ranger, navigator, hunter  
   \- Place in World: Wilderness guides, border patrols, hunters

3\. Thief  
   \- Stat Requirements: AGI 12+, INT 10+  
   \- Key Abilities: Lockpicking, stealth, trap disarming  
   \- Role in Party: Infiltrator, skill monkey  
   \- Place in World: Burglars, spies, members of thieves' guilds

4\. Acolyte  
   \- Stat Requirements: SPI 12+, INT 10+  
   \- Key Abilities: Divine magic, healing, religious lore  
   \- Role in Party: Healer, support caster  
   \- Place in World: Priests-in-training, temple assistants

5\. Hedge Wizard  
   \- Stat Requirements: INT 12+, SPI 10+  
   \- Key Abilities: Minor spells, potion brewing, magical lore  
   \- Role in Party: Utility caster, knowledge base  
   \- Place in World: Village wise-people, magical craftsmen

6\. Merchant  
   \- Stat Requirements: INT 10+, SPI 10+  
   \- Key Abilities: Negotiation, appraisal, local knowledge  
   \- Role in Party: Face, logistics  
   \- Place in World: Shopkeepers, caravan leaders, market vendors

Tier 3: Skilled Classes

1\. Warrior  
   \- Stat Requirements: STR 12+, STA 12+, AGI 10+  
   \- Key Abilities: Advanced combat techniques, weapon mastery, intimidation  
   \- Role in Party: Main damage dealer, tank  
   \- Place in World: Elite soldiers, gladiators, mercenary leaders

2\. Ranger  
   \- Stat Requirements: AGI 12+, INT 12+, STA 10+  
   \- Key Abilities: Expert tracking, animal handling, wilderness survival  
   \- Role in Party: Scout, archer, nature expert  
   \- Place in World: Forest wardens, monster hunters, wilderness guides

3\. Rogue  
   \- Stat Requirements: AGI 14+, INT 12+  
   \- Key Abilities: Expert stealth, acrobatics, dirty fighting  
   \- Role in Party: Skill specialist, sneak attacker  
   \- Place in World: Master thieves, assassins, spymasters

4\. Priest  
   \- Stat Requirements: SPI 14+, INT 12+  
   \- Key Abilities: Divine spells, exorcism, leadership  
   \- Role in Party: Healer, buffer, undead specialist  
   \- Place in World: Religious leaders, missionaries, temple guardians

5\. Mage  
   \- Stat Requirements: INT 14+, SPI 12+  
   \- Key Abilities: Arcane spells, magical research, artifact creation  
   \- Role in Party: Damage dealer, utility caster  
   \- Place in World: Court wizards, magical researchers, arcane artisans

6\. Bard  
   \- Stat Requirements: INT 12+, SPI 12+, AGI 10+  
   \- Key Abilities: Performance, inspiration, lore mastery  
   \- Role in Party: Support, face, jack-of-all-trades  
   \- Place in World: Wandering minstrels, court entertainers, loremasters
