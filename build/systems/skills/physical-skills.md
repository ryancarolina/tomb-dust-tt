# Physical Skills

All checks: **d20 + ability mod + PB + skill bonus** vs DC. Skill bonus from [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md). Duration, die, and cap effects use **skill level** (1–10).

---

## 1. Athletics (STR)

**Check:** d20 + STR mod + PB + Athletics skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** +5% movement speed per 2 levels; +5% carrying capacity per level.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Burst of Speed:** Once per day, double movement for 1 round.
- **Level 6 — Mighty Leap:** Add skill level to jump distance/height (feet).
- **Level 9 — Peak Performance:** Once per day, automatically succeed on one Athletics check.

---

## 2. Acrobatics (AGI)

**Check:** d20 + AGI mod + PB + Acrobatics skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** Reduce falling damage by 1d6 per 2 levels.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Tumble:** Move through enemy spaces without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Level 6 — Cat's Grace:** Always land on your feet when falling.
- **Level 9 — Impossible Acrobatics:** Once per day, automatically succeed on one Acrobatics check.

---

## 3. Stealth (AGI)

**Check:** d20 + AGI mod + PB + Stealth skill bonus vs observer's Perception

**Skill level effects:** Move at 50% speed while hidden + 5% per level (max 100% at level 10).

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Shadow Blend:** Advantage on Stealth in dim light or darkness.
- **Level 6 — Silent Step:** No movement penalty while stealthing.
- **Level 9 — Vanish:** Once per day, become invisible for 1d4 rounds.

---

## 4. Sleight of Hand (AGI)

**Check:** d20 + AGI mod + PB + Sleight of Hand skill bonus vs Perception

**Skill level effects:** Pickpocket value limit 5 gp × skill level without extra DC.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Fast Hands:** One Sleight of Hand check as a free action per round.
- **Level 6 — Misdirection:** Advantage when you have a distraction.
- **Level 9 — Perfect Theft:** Once per day, automatically succeed on one Sleight of Hand check.

---

## 5. Endurance (STA)

**Check:** d20 + STA mod + PB + Endurance skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** Extend time before fatigue by 10% per level.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Second Wind:** Once per day, recover 2d6 HP as a free action.
- **Level 6 — Iron Stomach:** Advantage on saves vs poison and disease.
- **Level 9 — Tireless:** Ignore fatigue effects for 1 hour per day.
