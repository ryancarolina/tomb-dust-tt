# Magic Skills

Spell math uses [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md). **Skill bonus** on rolls; **skill level** for MP pool bumps and technique scaling.

---

## 1. Spellcasting (INT)

**Description:** Proficiency in casting and controlling magic.

**Spell attack:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Spellcasting skill bonus vs AC  
**Spell save DC:** 8 + casting mod + PB + Spellcasting skill bonus (+1 if Spell Focus matches spell school). Arcane = INT; divine = SPI ([combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md)).

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Spell Focus:** +1 to spell attack rolls and save DC for one chosen school of magic.
- **Level 6 — Mana Efficiency:** Reduce MP cost of spells by 1 (minimum 1 MP).
- **Level 9 — Arcane Mastery:** Once per day, gain **advantage** on one spell attack roll or impose **disadvantage** on one target's save against your spell.

---

## 2. Magical Defense (SPI)

**Description:** Resist and defend against magical attacks.

**SPI save vs magic:** d20 + SPI mod + PB + Magical Defense skill bonus vs effect DC  
**AC vs spell attack:** normal AC + Magical Defense skill bonus (spells only)

See [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md#magical-defense-spi).

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Spell Resistance:** +2 to saves and AC vs one chosen school of magic.
- **Level 6 — Magical Barrier:** Once per day, add your SPI modifier to Magical Defense for 1 round.
- **Level 9 — Spell Deflection:** Once per day, on a successful defense against a spell, redirect it to a target within 30 ft (they save normally).

---

## 3. Mana Control (INT)

**Max MP:** Base MP + (INT × 3) + (Mana Control skill level × 2)  
**MP regeneration per turn:** SPI mod + Mana Control skill level (minimum 0)

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Mana Tap:** Once per day, instantly recover INT mod + Mana Control skill level MP.
- **Level 6 — Efficient Casting:** Once per day, cast one spell without spending MP.
- **Level 9 — Mana Mastery:** Once per day, for 1 minute all spells you cast cost 1 MP.

---

## 4. Magical Knowledge (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Magical Knowledge skill bonus vs DC

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Spell Identification:** Identify a spell being cast with a successful check (DC set by GM or spell tier).
- **Level 6 — Magical Insight:** Create minor magical items or temporary enchantments (DC 15+ Magical Knowledge).
- **Level 9 — Arcane Secrets:** Once per week, attempt to create a major magical item (DC 20+).
