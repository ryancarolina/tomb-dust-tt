# Mental Skills

All checks: **d20 + ability mod + PB + skill bonus** vs DC. Skill bonus from [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md).

---

## 1. Investigation (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Investigation skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** Find 1 + (skill level ÷ 2) clues on a thorough successful check.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Keen Eye:** Advantage to find hidden objects.
- **Level 6 — Rapid Analysis:** Cut investigation time by 25%.
- **Level 9 — Eureka Moment:** Once per day, automatically succeed on one Investigation check.

---

## 2. Perception (SPI)

**Check:** d20 + SPI mod + PB + Perception skill bonus vs DC  
**Passive Perception:** 10 + SPI mod + Perception skill bonus

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Alertness:** Cannot be surprised while conscious.
- **Level 6 — Danger Sense:** Advantage to detect traps and ambushes.
- **Level 9 — Eagle Eye:** Double visual and auditory range.

---

## 3. Lore (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Lore skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** Recall basic facts about (skill level × 2) topics you've studied.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Savant:** Choose a specialty; advantage on Lore in that area.
- **Level 6 — Obscure Knowledge:** Once per day, recall an obscure fact granting advantage on a related check.
- **Level 9 — Living Library:** Attempt Lore checks on any topic at −2 if untrained in that topic.

---

## 4. Arcana (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Arcana skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** Identify spells of tier up to (skill level ÷ 2, round up) on success.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Magical Insight:** Advantage to identify magical items.
- **Level 6 — Counterspell:** Once per day, attempt to counter a spell as a reaction (opposed Arcana or spellcasting check vs DC 10 + spell tier).
- **Level 9 — Arcane Mastery:** Attempt to use magic items without class restrictions (Arcana check DC 15+).

---

## 5. Medicine (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Medicine skill bonus vs DC  
**Healing (out of combat):** Restore 1d4 + skill level HP on a successful check (10 minutes; once per target per short rest).

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Field Medic:** Stabilize a dying creature as a bonus action.
- **Level 6 — Treat Disease:** Advantage on checks to treat disease and poison.
- **Level 9 — Miraculous Recovery:** Once per day, restore a creature to half max HP as an action.

---

## 6. Nature (SPI)

**Check:** d20 + SPI mod + PB + Nature skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** Forage for (skill level) people per day on success.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Animal Whisperer:** Advantage to calm or influence animals.
- **Level 6 — Weather Sense:** Predict weather for 24 hours.
- **Level 9 — One with Nature:** Communicate simple ideas with plants and animals.

---

## 7. Engineering (INT)

**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Engineering skill bonus vs DC

**Skill level effects:** Reduce build/repair time by 5% per level.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Quick Fix:** Temporarily repair a device or structure as an action.
- **Level 6 — Improved Design:** Devices you build perform 10% above standard.
- **Level 9 — Master Engineer:** Once per week, automatically succeed on one Engineering check.
