# Combat Skills

Standard attack: **d20 + ability mod + PB + skill bonus vs AC**.  
Standard weapon damage: **weapon die + ability mod + skill bonus**.  
See [skill-checks.md](skill-checks.md) and [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md).

---

## 1. Swordsmanship (STR)

**Description:** Proficiency with all types of swords.

**Attack:** d20 + STR mod + PB + Swordsmanship skill bonus vs AC  
**Damage:** sword damage + STR mod + Swordsmanship skill bonus

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Precision Strike:** Ignore 2 AC on this attack.
- **Level 6 — Whirlwind Attack:** Attack all adjacent enemies; each attack at −2.
- **Level 9 — Vital Strike:** On a critical hit, add your Swordsmanship skill bonus to damage again.

---

## 2. Axe Mastery (STR)

**Attack:** d20 + STR mod + PB + Axe Mastery skill bonus vs AC  
**Damage:** axe damage + STR mod + Axe Mastery skill bonus

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Rending Strike:** Ignore 2 AC on this attack.
- **Level 6 — Cleaving Blow:** On a hit that beats AC by 3+, deal half damage (no ability mod) to one adjacent enemy.
- **Level 9 — Berserker Fury:** Make one additional axe attack as a bonus action at −2.

---

## 3. Mace and Flail Expertise (STR)

**Attack:** d20 + STR mod + PB + Mace and Flail Expertise skill bonus vs AC  
**Damage:** weapon damage + STR mod + skill bonus

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Bone Crusher:** Ignore 2 AC on this attack.
- **Level 6 — Crushing Blow:** On a critical hit, target makes a STA save (DC 8 + your PB + STR mod) or is **Stunned** until end of your next turn.
- **Level 9 — Flail Mastery:** Attacks with flails ignore shield AC bonus.

---

## 4. Polearm Proficiency (STR)

**Attack:** d20 + STR mod + PB + Polearm Proficiency skill bonus vs AC  
**Damage:** polearm damage + STR mod + skill bonus

**Passive:** +1 AC when wielding a polearm and alert. Can attack enemies 2 squares away.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Thrust:** Ignore 2 AC on this attack.
- **Level 6 — Sweep:** Attack all enemies in a 180° arc in front of you; each attack at −2.
- **Level 9 — Set Against Charge:** Double damage against a charging enemy.

---

## 5. Archery (AGI)

**Attack:** d20 + AGI mod + PB + Archery skill bonus vs AC  
**Damage:** arrow damage + AGI mod + Archery skill bonus

**Passive:** Effective range +10% per 2 skill levels.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Precise Shot:** Ignore half cover against the target.
- **Level 6 — Rapid Shot:** Fire two arrows as one action; each at −2.
- **Level 9 — Penetrating Shot:** On a hit, make a second attack roll at −2 against a second target in line behind the first.

---

## 6. Crossbow Expertise (AGI)

**Attack:** d20 + AGI mod + PB + Crossbow Expertise skill bonus vs AC  
**Damage:** bolt damage + AGI mod + Crossbow Expertise skill bonus

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Quick Load:** Reload costs one less action (minimum bonus action).
- **Level 6 — Piercing Bolt:** Ignore 2 AC from armor and shield on this shot.
- **Level 9 — Multi-Shot:** Fire three bolts as one action; each at −2.

---

## 7. Throwing Weapons (AGI)

**Attack:** d20 + AGI mod + PB + Throwing Weapons skill bonus vs AC  
**Damage:** weapon damage + AGI mod + skill bonus

**Passive:** Thrown range +15% per skill level.

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Quick Draw:** Draw and throw as a single action.
- **Level 6 — Dual Throw:** Throw two weapons as one action; each at −2.
- **Level 9 — Return Throw:** Once per combat, a thrown weapon returns to your hand after the attack.

---

## 8. Unarmed Combat (STR)

**Attack:** d20 + STR mod + PB + Unarmed Combat skill bonus vs AC  
**Damage:** unarmed die + STR mod + skill bonus (see die upgrades below)

**Grapple:** contested d20 + STR mod + PB + Unarmed Combat skill bonus vs opponent's d20 + STR mod + PB + Unarmed Combat or Athletics skill bonus

**Die upgrades (skill level):** 1d4 base → **1d6** at level 3 → **1d8** at level 9

**Techniques**
- **Level 6 — Stunning Fist:** On a critical hit, target makes STA save (DC 8 + PB + STR mod) or is **Stunned** until end of your next turn.

---

## 9. Martial Arts (AGI)

**Attack:** d20 + AGI mod + PB + Martial Arts skill bonus vs AC  
**Damage:** unarmed die + STR mod + skill bonus

**Die upgrades (skill level):** 1d4 base → 1d6 at level 2 → 1d8 at level 6 → 1d10 at level 10

**Techniques**
- **Level 4 — Disabling Strike:** On a critical hit, target's AGI score is treated as 2 lower for 2 rounds.
- **Level 8 — Flowing Strikes:** On a critical hit, make one additional unarmed attack.

---

## 10. Shield Use (STR)

**Passive:** When wielding a shield and alert, use your **reaction** when hit to roll d20 + STR mod + PB + Shield Use skill bonus vs the attack roll. On success, reduce damage by **1 + skill level** (minimum 1).

**Techniques**
- **Level 5 — Shield Bash:** Shield bash: d20 + STR mod + PB + skill bonus vs AC; 1d4 + STR bludgeoning on hit.
- **Level 8 — Deflection:** Once per combat, automatically succeed on one shield block reaction.
- **Level 10 — Phalanx:** Adjacent allies gain +2 AC while you wield a shield and remain alert.

---

## 11. Dodge (AGI)

**Formula:** See [combat/calculations.md](../combat/calculations.md) (Dodge skill table). **No benefit when flat-footed.**

**Techniques**
- **Level 5 — Evasion:** When you use Dodge disadvantage against an area effect, take half damage on a failed save instead of full.
- **Level 8 — Uncanny Dodge:** Once per long rest, act alert for Dodge and AGI to AC even if surprised.
- **Level 10 — Superior Evasion:** When Evasion applies and you succeed on the save, take no damage.

---

## 12. Armor Proficiency (STA)

**Passive:** Reduce armor movement penalty by 1 per 2 skill levels (minimum 0). +1 AC per 3 skill levels while wearing armor and alert (max +3 at level 9–10).

**Techniques**
- **Level 5 — Second Skin:** Halve remaining armor Stealth/disadvantage penalties.
- **Level 8 — Indomitable:** Once per day, treat one critical hit against you as a normal hit.
- **Level 10 — Living Fortress:** Reduce incoming weapon damage by 2 while wearing heavy armor.

---

## 13. Dual Wielding (AGI)

**Main hand:** d20 + AGI mod + PB + Dual Wielding skill bonus vs AC  
**Off hand:** d20 + AGI mod + PB + half Dual Wielding skill bonus (round down) vs AC  
**Off-hand damage:** weapon die + half skill bonus (round down)

**Techniques**
- **Level 3 — Flurry:** Once per combat, make one additional off-hand attack.
- **Level 6 — Balanced Grip:** Off-hand attacks use full skill bonus.
- **Level 9 — Whirlwind Strike:** Attack all adjacent enemies once with each weapon (each at −2).

---

## 14. Battlefield Awareness (INT)

**Initiative:** +1 per 2 skill levels (round down).  
**Check:** d20 + INT mod + PB + Battlefield Awareness skill bonus vs DC

**Techniques**
- **Level 5 / 10:** Once per combat, reposition one ally within 30 ft as a free action.

---

## 15. Power Attack (STR)

Trade attack accuracy for damage **before** rolling.

**Attack:** d20 + STR mod + PB + weapon skill bonus − penalty vs AC  
**Damage:** weapon damage + STR mod + (penalty × 2)

Maximum penalty = Power Attack **skill level** (e.g. level 5 → up to −5/+10).

**Skill level caps:** 1 → max −1; 3 → −3; 5 → −5; 7 → −7; 10 → −10.
