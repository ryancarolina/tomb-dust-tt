# Death and persistence

Tomb Dust treats **character death** as expected, not exceptional. A delver who dies on a stamped map becomes **Tomb Dust**—ash scraped from the site, a name in a Registry ledger, and (if the party is lucky) salvage someone else carries out. The **campaign** keeps moving: maps stay marked, factions remember, and the next body on the contract inherits the mess.

This is the account-level contract between players and the setting. For the economy and run structure that produce these outcomes, see [world/extraction.md](../world/extraction.md).

---

## What resets on death

When a PC **dies during a delve** (0 HP and no recovery before the scene closes, or GM-declared fatality). In-combat **Dying** and stabilization: [combat/encounter.md](../combat/encounter.md).

| Category | Resets |
|----------|--------|
| **Character** | Name, attributes, class tier, deeds, skill XP, HP, conditions, Fortune (**LUC**) pool for that character |
| **Gear on body** | Worn armor, wielded weapons, carried pack, coin purse, map case on person, consumables not yet in stash |
| **Run state** | Unextracted salvage in their hands, temporary buffs, “this delver’s” oaths and licenses tied to the corpse |

**On the map:** the body, everything it was wearing, and anything only that PC was carrying stay **in the site** until another party recovers them—or they are lost to collapse, beasts, or rot.

**Not a reset:** another PC extracting the dead delver’s tagged heirloom counts as a successful recovery for the **account** (see below), not a resurrection of the character.

## What persists (account / campaign)

These belong to the **player’s delver line** (successor characters, heirs, or “the same player at the table”), not to the corpse:

| Persists | Definition |
|----------|------------|
| **Registry log** | Official record: stamps bought, survivor registrations, deaths filed by **AV-GRID**, insurance claims, outstanding fines. Clerks know the **name on the contract**, not the face—successors inherit the paper trail. |
| **Map knowledge** | Player-learned truth about a site: traps cleared, doors opened, veil behavior, short rests used, collapse progress. Represent as shared notes, marked map copies, or Registry **survey amendments**—not automatic GPS. |
| **Faction reputation** | Standing with Registry, realms, Iron Pact, Verdant Vale, patrons, fences, and contacts built over multiple characters. Betrayals and heroics stick to the **account**, not the dead delver’s sheet. |
| **Account stash** | Gear and coin **deposited at a safe hub** before ingress (Registry strongbox, patron vault, licensed fence escrow, home lockup in a Registry town). Stash is **not** on the body; it survives death. |

### Account stash (clear boundary)

**In stash:** weapons and armor stored between runs, extracted loot not taken on the current delve, debt ledger balance, heirlooms locked in town, spellbooks left with a clerk.

**Not in stash:** anything the PC chose to carry through the **ingress** threshold for this run. If it is on the sheet when they cross in, it is **body loot** until extracted to a hub.

**Hub rule:** only locations the table agrees are safe and Registry-recognised (e.g. Edgecombe clerk’s strongroom, Breley licensed quartermaster) count. A camp **inside** `47-B-UG-3` is not a stash.

## New character inheritance

When a player brings a **new PC** after a death (or retires a survivor), the successor **does not** copy the old character sheet. They **do** inherit the campaign’s extraction progress:

| Inherited | Typical form |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Debt** | Unpaid stamp fees, gear loans, patron advances, insurance premiums, ash tithe arrears—Registry clerks attach debt to the **contract holder** (player account), not the corpse. |
| **Contacts** | Fence who trusts you, Mira’s survey queue, knight license interviews waived, thieves’ guild vouch—transfer as fiction; same NPC attitudes unless the table burned the bridge. |
| **World progress** | Cleared map annotations, faction rep, stash contents, open survey bounties (e.g. `47-B-UG-3` re-survey), party discoveries other PCs extracted |

**Starting tier:** new characters begin at **Tier 1** (creation defaults) unless the table uses a specific deed reward to skip ahead—**deeds die with the character** unless explicitly logged as **household or account deeds** (patron writ, Registry commendation). When in doubt, inherit **opportunity**, not **stats**.

**Insurance (optional):** [Delver's Registry](../factions/delvers-registry.md) pool (e.g. 15 gp premium) may pay heirs **50 gp + one recovered personal item (GM)** on a stamped TPK—see [Mira Ashret](../npcs/mira-ashret.md). This is not revival; it is paperwork and a token.

## Recovery runs and Tomb Dust

A later delve can target a **prior death site** to recover:

- Tagged heirlooms (account progress)
- Registry-insured personal items
- Map cases or survey notes left on corpses
- Bodies for proper burial (ash tithe, faction requirements)

Failure on a recovery run adds more names to the log; success does not restore the old PC.

---

## Tone link

Hardcore extraction means **risk is priced, death is logged, and progress is shared**. Players learn routes, manage stash vs. loadout, and accept that the delver is disposable—the **map**, the **Rep**, and the **debt** are not. That is the social meaning of Tomb Dust: you are ash; the Registry entry outlives you.
