# Eclipse Festival

Description: A bi-annual event celebrating the balance between Aven and Thar during a solar eclipse.  
Activities: Rituals, feasts, and competitions in honor of the gods.  
Significance: A time of unity where different races and factions come together to celebrate and share knowledge.  
Challenges: Potential for conflict as old grudges surface during the gathering.

**World ties:** Truce week across factions ([world/aventhar-overview.md](../world/aventhar-overview.md)). Host cities rotate: Breley, Boydon, Frostfall (by year).
