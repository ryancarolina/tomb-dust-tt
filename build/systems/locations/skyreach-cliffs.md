# Skyreach Cliffs

Overview: Towering cliffs that reach into the clouds, home to the Skyreach Monastery.  
Culture and Society: Monks and scholars dedicated to the study of the stars and the Ether.  
Economy and Resources: Rare herbs and minerals found only at high altitudes, traded for magical artifacts.  
Challenges and Threats: Frequent lightning storms and aerial predators like Roc birds.  
Role in Aventhar: A place of enlightenment and refuge, offering guidance and rare knowledge.
