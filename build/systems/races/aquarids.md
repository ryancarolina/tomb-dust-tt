# Aquarids

Origins: Born from the deep seas, Aquarids are humanoid beings with gills and webbed limbs.  
Culture and Society: Underwater cities made of coral and bioluminescent flora. They value harmony with marine life.  
Abilities: Exceptional swimmers, able to communicate with sea creatures and manipulate water.  
Role in Aventhar: Guardians of the seas, ensuring the balance of marine ecosystems and protecting against underwater threats.  
Challenges: Facing threats from abyssal creatures and pollution from the surface.
