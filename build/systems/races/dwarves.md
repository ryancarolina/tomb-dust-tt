# Dwarves

Origins: Born in the heart of Aventhar's mountains, Dwarves are the undisputed masters of craftsmanship and mining. Their societies are built on the principles of honor and loyalty.

Culture & Society: Dwarven cities are marvels of engineering and artistry, reflecting a deep respect for tradition and the materials they coax from the earth.

Abilities: Renowned for their skill in metallurgy and stonecraft, Dwarves create artifacts of unmatched quality, and their warriors are formidable in their steadfastness and strength.

Role in Aventhar: As defenders of the deep places and keepers of ancient knowledge, Dwarves contribute to the realm's stability and richness, sharing their creations and wisdom.

Challenges: Their insular nature and focus on tradition can sometimes hinder adaptation to the realm's changing dynamics, and their treasures often attract unwelcome attention from greed-driven forces.
