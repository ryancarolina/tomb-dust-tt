# Centaurs

Origins: Centaurs roam Aventhar's plains and forests, embodying the wild majesty of the natural world and living in close harmony with the land that sustains them.

Culture & Society: Organized into tribes, Centaurs value freedom, family, and the stewardship of nature. Their lives are a seamless blend of tradition and the untamed wild.

Abilities: Renowned for their speed, strength, and wisdom, Centaurs are unmatched in their knowledge of the natural world and serve as protectors and guides.

Role in Aventhar: They guard Aventhar's most sacred places, ensuring that the balance of nature is respected and preserved. They are also standing ready to defend the realm's natural wonders.

Challenges: Civilization's encroachment and disruption of natural harmony threaten their way of life, compelling them to defend their lands and the balance of nature against growing external pressures.
