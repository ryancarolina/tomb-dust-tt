# Hollow Knight

## Lore

**Origin:** Fallen knights interred without Aven’s rite; armor animates with echo of duty and no face inside the helm.

**Description:** Empty plate that moves as if occupied; voice like wind in a helm.

**Weaknesses:** Remove helm (AC drops to 14, loses Turn Command); speak their oath to force 1 round hesitation (GM).
