# Grave Ghouls

## Lore

**Origin:** Corpses left in Ether-touched tombs without rites; hunger for warm flesh and remembered names.

**Description:** Hunched, dirt-caked dead with too many teeth and tomb-clay under their nails.

**Weaknesses:** Consecration; decapitation prevents **Rise Again** (below).

---

## Stat blocks

### Grave Ghoul (Skirmisher)
| | |
| **AC** | 13 |
| **HP** | 28 |
| **Move** | 8 |
| **PB** | +2 |

| STR | AGI | STA | INT | SPI |
| +2 | +2 | +2 | −2 | −1 |

**Traits** **Rise Again.** When reduced to 0 HP, DC 13 consecration or decapitation within 1 round or returns to 1d6 HP once.

**Downed behavior:** **feast** — prefers Downed and Dying delvers; keeps clawing until they stop moving.

**Actions**
- **Claw.** +5 to hit. *Hit:* 1d6+3 slashing + 1d4 necrotic; DC 12 STA save or **Paralyzed** until end of next turn (one target per ghoul per round).

**Tactics** Swarm wounded; drag bodies into side tunnels.

**Treasure** Burial goods (5–20 gp), tomb map scrap.

---

### Hungry Pack (Hazard, swarm)
Treat as **4 HP each**, 6 minions per “swarm” (AC 12, +3 to hit, 1d4+1 damage). Destroying 4 minions breaks the swarm’s surround.
