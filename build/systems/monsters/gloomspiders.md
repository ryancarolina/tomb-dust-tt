# Gloomspiders

## Lore

**Origin:** Darkest depths of the Ether, where fear coalesces. Drawn to sorrow, decay, tombs, and deep caves.

**Description:** Huge shadow-wrapped spiders; eyes glow red or green. Webs are nearly invisible, steel-strong, and soul-tainting.

**Abilities (lore):** **Soul webs** drain life force; **shadow camouflage** in darkness.

**Weaknesses:** **Light magic** reveals and weakens them; **fire** destroys webs and burns the body.
