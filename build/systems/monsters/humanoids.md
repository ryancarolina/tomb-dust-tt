# Humanoid templates

Generic **skirmisher-tier** stat blocks for guards, cultists, and bandits. Use when a location names "Registry guard" or "Thar cultist" without a unique NPC sheet.

All attacks: **d20 + listed bonus vs AC**. Saves: **DC 8 + PB + mod**.
