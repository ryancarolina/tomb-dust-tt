# Sewer Spider

## Lore

**Origin:** Mundane giant spiders nesting in Breley underworks; drawn to damp stone and trapped prey.

**Description:** Plate-sized bodies with mottled brown chitin; webs of sticky silk, not Ether-tainted.

**Weaknesses:** Fire destroys webs; bright lantern light reveals ceiling ambushes. Unlike Ether **Gloomspider** broodlings, sewer spiders deal piercing damage only — no necrotic rider, soul web, or shadow camouflage.

**Traits** **Spider Climb.** Can climb difficult surfaces including ceilings without a check.

**Downed behavior:** **ignore** — ambushes active prey; does not finish Downed delvers (tutorial safety).

**Actions**
- **Bite.** +4 to hit. *Hit:* 1d4+2 piercing.
- **Web (Recharge 5–6).** Ranged 20 ft, +4 to hit. *Hit:* target is **Webbed** (speed 0). Escape: DC 11 STR or AGI check as action.

**Tactics** Ceiling ambush; web to split or slow the party; retreat when webs are burned. Mundane Breley infestation — not an Ether or shadow predator.

**Treasure** Spider silk (5 gp), scrap (1–3 gp).
