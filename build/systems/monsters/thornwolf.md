# Thornwolf

## Lore

**Origin:** Whispering Woods predators—wolves twisted by Ether saplings growing through their hides.

**Description:** Wolf silhouettes bristling with thorny branches; eyes like green lantern oil.

**Weaknesses:** Fire on brambles (disadvantage on attacks 1 round); cannot enter consecrated groves.
