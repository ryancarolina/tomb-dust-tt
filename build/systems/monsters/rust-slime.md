# Rust Slime

## Lore

**Origin:** Dungeon ooze feeding on old iron; common in Breley vault underlevels and tomb crypts.

**Description:** Orange-brown gelatin with suspended metal flakes; dissolves blades over time.

**Weaknesses:** Cold slows; no metal touch (poles, magic).
