# Shadowkin

## Lore

**Origin:** Servants bred in Thar’s under-realms and the Black Vale—humanoids fused with living shadow. The Knights of the Ninety-Ninth Night still hunt warbands slipping through sealed rifts.

**Description:** Lean, gray-skinned figures with no cast shadow (their shadow walks beside them as a separate predator). Eyes like wet obsidian; weapons of black glass.

**Abilities (lore):** **Merge with shadow** for ambush; **command lesser shades**; resistant to fear.

**Weaknesses:** **Bright light** suppresses merge; **Aven-blessed** weapons deal normal damage (they resist nonmagical weapons otherwise).
