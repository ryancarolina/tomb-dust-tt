# Ether Larva

## Lore

**Origin:** Spawn shed by Aetherial Beasts and thin-veil ruptures; vermin of the magical wild.

**Description:** Palm-sized crawling rainbows that bite with static teeth.

**Weaknesses:** Salt circle; dies in 1 round if separated from Ether source farther than 100 ft.
