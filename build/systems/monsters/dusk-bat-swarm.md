# Dusk Bat Swarm

## Lore

**Origin:** Caves and ruined towers at twilight; colonies awaken when blood is spilled nearby.

**Description:** Cloud of fist-sized bats; sound like rustling cloth; smell of iron.

**Weaknesses:** Area fire; bright light (disadvantage on attacks).

**Traits** **Swarm.** Occupies one space; resistant to bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from non-area attacks. **Echolocate.** Not blind in darkness.

**Actions**

**Tactics** Drop from ceiling on lit corridors; flee fire.
