# Mirelings

## Lore

**Origin:** Said to be Thar’s test for the proud—born of swamp deceit where land and water blur.

**Description:** Humanoid shapeshifters mirroring victims until the trap closes.

**Abilities (lore):** **Mimicry** of appearance and voice; **swamp camouflage** in bog and reeds.

**Weaknesses:** **True name** compels truth or obedience; **salt and iron** break mimicry and wound them.
