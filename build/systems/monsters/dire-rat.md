# Dire Rat

## Lore

**Origin:** Breley sewer vermin grown fat on refuse and runoff; plague carriers in the underworks.

**Description:** Dog-sized rats with yellowed fangs and patchy fur matted with slime.

**Weaknesses:** Fire and loud noise scatter packs; narrow gratings block pursuit.
